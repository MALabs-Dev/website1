About Shoba-e-Banin

شعبۂ بنین کا تعارف

Shoba-e-Banin is the boys' department of Jamia Ayesha Siddiqua, offering a structured, rigorous, and spiritually enriching education for male students of all age groups. The department follows a carefully designed curriculum that integrates classical Islamic sciences with relevant modern knowledge.

Students in Shoba-e-Banin benefit from dedicated male faculty, a disciplined and focused environment, and a culture that emphasises brotherhood, mutual respect, and academic excellence rooted in Islamic values.

  • Structured daily schedule including Fajr Salah and Quran recitation
  • Regular Tajweed and Hifz sessions with qualified Huffaz
  • Arabic and Urdu language development integrated throughout the curriculum
  • Regular assessments, examinations, and progress reporting
  • Extracurricular Islamic activities: debates, qira'at competitions, and more
  • Computer education and vocational skills alongside Deeni studies

Shoba-e-Banat

شعبۂ بنات — لڑکیوں کا شعبہ

A safe, nurturing, and high-quality Islamic educational department dedicated to female students

About Shoba-e-Banat

شعبۂ بنات کا تعارف

Shoba-e-Banat is the girls' department of Jamia Ayesha Siddiqua — a dedicated, secure, and empowering educational space for female students. The department is led by experienced female scholars and educators who are committed to providing the highest standard of Islamic education in a completely appropriate and supportive environment.

Students in Shoba-e-Banat receive a comprehensive education that prepares them to be knowledgeable Muslimahs, capable of serving their families and communities with wisdom, integrity, and grace. The curriculum includes the full range of Islamic sciences alongside modern practical skills.

  • Fully female faculty and administration — a completely secure environment
  • Comprehensive Hifz program with expert female Huffaz
  • Alimah and Fazilah courses following classical Islamic methodology
  • Momina Course for foundational Islamic knowledge and practice
  • Tailoring, embroidery, and skill development for economic empowerment
  • Regular parent-teacher communication and progress updates

Admission Rules & Requirements

1

Age Requirement: Students must be at least 6–7 years old for the Hifz program and 10+ for the Alimah/Fazilah courses. Age requirements vary by program — please enquire for specific course eligibility.

2

Basic Quran Knowledge: Applicants for the Hifz and Alimah programs should have a basic ability to read the Quran (Nazira). Those without this foundation may be enrolled in a preparatory program first.

3

Documents Required: Birth certificate or age proof, recent passport-size photographs (2), previous educational certificates (if any), and a parent/guardian's ID proof.

4

Interview: All applicants must attend an interview at the Jamia with their parent or guardian. The interview assesses basic reading ability, aptitude, and suitability for the chosen program.

5

Guardian Commitment: Parents or guardians must commit to supporting the student's regular attendance, punctuality, and compliance with Jamia rules and code of conduct.

6

Dress Code: Students are expected to follow the Islamic dress code as per the Jamia's guidelines. Details will be provided at the time of admission.

7

Fee Structure: The Jamia strives to keep fees as low as possible to make Islamic education accessible to all. Fee concessions and scholarships are available for deserving students — contact us to enquire.

8

Conduct & Discipline: Students are expected to maintain the highest standards of Islamic conduct. The Jamia reserves the right to dismiss students whose behaviour is found to be contrary to Islamic values and the institution's code of conduct.

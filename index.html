About Jamia Ayesha Siddiqua

علم حاصل کرنا ہر مسلمان مرد اور عورت پر فرض ہے

Jamia Ayesha Siddiqua Lil Banin Wal Banat is a premier Islamic educational institution located in Patancheru, Hyderabad, Telangana. Founded under the guidance of Maulana Muhammad Saleem Rahmani, the Jamia is dedicated to providing comprehensive Deeni education to both boys and girls.

  • Separate departments for boys (Shoba-e-Banin) and girls (Shoba-e-Banat)
  • Authentic Islamic curriculum rooted in the Quran and Sunnah
  • Modern skills including Computer Education and Tailoring
  • Safe, nurturing, and spiritually enriching environment
  • Qualified, experienced, and dedicated teaching staff

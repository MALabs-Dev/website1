Hifz-ul-Quran

حفظ القرآن الکریم
Duration3–5 Years
Age7+ Years
DepartmentBanin & Banat
Apply Now

About the Hifz Program

The Hifz-ul-Quran program is the foundation of our institution. Students who complete this sacred journey memorise the entire Holy Quran — all 30 Juz — under the supervision of qualified, experienced Huffaz. The program follows a structured revision and memorisation schedule ensuring lasting retention.

Our Hifz curriculum also integrates proper Tajweed (rules of recitation) so students not only memorise the Quran but recite it correctly and beautifully. Regular revision sessions, individual attention, and monthly progress assessments ensure every student progresses at an effective pace.

✅ Qualified Huffaz teachers
✅ Individual attention & tracking
✅ Tajweed integrated
✅ Monthly parent reports
✅ Regular revision sessions
✅ Completion ceremony

About the Alimah & Fazilah Program

The Alimah and Fazilah courses are the flagship advanced Islamic scholarship programs of Jamia Ayesha Siddiqua, offered primarily through the Shoba-e-Banat department. These programs cover the full spectrum of classical Islamic sciences over several years of rigorous study.

The Alimah course provides a comprehensive foundation in Tafsir, Hadith, Fiqh, Aqeedah, Arabic grammar, and Islamic history. The Fazilah course builds upon this with advanced studies in specialised Islamic disciplines, producing graduates who are qualified to teach and guide their communities.

✅ Tafsir & Quran sciences
✅ Hadith with chain analysis
✅ Fiqh & Islamic jurisprudence
✅ Arabic grammar & literature
✅ Islamic history & biography
✅ Recognised certification
Available for: 🌸 Shoba-e-Banat

Age12+ Years
DepartmentShoba-e-Banat

About the Momina Course

The Momina Course is a focused Islamic program for girls and young women who wish to strengthen their faith, Islamic knowledge, and character. It is shorter than the full Alimah course but provides a solid, practical grounding in all essential aspects of Islamic belief and practice.

This course is ideal for students who may not be able to commit to the longer Alimah program but wish to gain the knowledge needed to be a practicing, knowledgeable Muslim woman — capable of raising a righteous family and contributing positively to their community.

✅ Quran with Tajweed
✅ Basic Fiqh & Ibadah
✅ Islamic character building
✅ Seerah & Islamic history
✅ Practical Islamic lifestyle
✅ Certificate on completion
Available for: 🌸 Shoba-e-Banat

About the One-Year Diploma in Deeniyat

The One-Year Diploma in Deeniyat is an intensive, accessible program designed for students who wish to acquire essential Islamic knowledge in a short time. It covers the core pillars of Islamic faith, worship, ethics, and practice in a structured and engaging way.

This diploma is particularly suitable for students combining religious and secular studies, adults returning to Islamic education, or anyone seeking a thorough grounding in their Deen without committing to a multi-year program.

✅ Quran recitation & basics
✅ Pillars of Islam & Iman
✅ Fiqh of daily worship
✅ Islamic morals & ethics
✅ Short Seerah overview
✅ Diploma certificate

About Computer Education

In recognition of the importance of digital literacy in the modern world, Jamia Ayesha Siddiqua offers a comprehensive Computer Education program for both boys and girls. This course ensures students are equipped with the essential technology skills needed for education, communication, and future employment.

The curriculum covers basic to intermediate computer skills, with an emphasis on responsible and ethical use of technology — consistent with Islamic values. Students learn to use productivity software, navigate the internet safely, and develop digital communication skills.

About Tailoring & Skill Development

The Tailoring and Skill Development program empowers female students with practical vocational skills that can provide them with economic independence and a means of halal livelihood. This program is an integral part of the holistic education offered at Jamia Ayesha Siddiqua.

Students learn garment cutting, stitching, embroidery, and basic fashion design under expert instructors. The course is designed to produce graduates who can run their own tailoring business, contribute to family income, or pursue further training in textile and fashion.

✅ Basic to advanced stitching
✅ Garment cutting & patterns
✅ Embroidery & handcraft
✅ Machine operation
✅ Practical project work
✅ Certificate on completion
